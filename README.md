## 🌟 Overview
Interactive 3D WebGL packaging proofing suite, engineering specifications, and 1:1 commercial production vector CAD dielines for **Princess Isabelle "Golden Silk Facial Mask"**.

- **Box Architecture:** Rigid Slide Drawer Box (CEILK Matchbox Style) with 15 mm Champagne Gold Double-Face Satin Ribbon pull tab.
- **Finished External Size:** 132 mm (W) × 174 mm (L) × 43 mm (H)
- **Inner Drawer Cavity:** 126 mm (W) × 168 mm (L) × 38 mm (D)
- **Pack Quantity:** 3 Complete Sets (Stacked)
- **Dual-Chamber System:** 
  - **Outer Sachet:** 120 × 160 mm (Foil Composite 114µ)
  - **Inner Serum Pouch:** 90 × 120 mm (30 ml Active Liquid Capacity)

---

## 🎮 Interactive 3D WebGL Simulator Features
- **360° Free Orbit & Zoom**: Inspect lighting, soft shadows, and metallic gold foil reflections.
- **Drawer Slide Slider (0% - 100%)**: Smoothly pull and close the inner drawer tray.
- **Preset Camera Angles**:
  - `Hero View`: Angled presentation of the partially open slide box.
  - `Inside Tray`: Top-down view into the inner drawer cavity.
  - `Exploded View`: Animated 3D breakout separating sleeve, drawer, and 3 sachet layers.
  - `360° Turntable`: Continuous rotation mode.
  - `Isolated Product Inspections`: Dedicated inspection of the 12×16 cm Outer Sachet and 9×12 cm Serum Pouch.

## 📦 Master Shipping Carton & Logistics
- **Pack Quantity:** 24 Box Sets / Carton
- **Arrangement:** 6 boxes per layer (2×3 grid) × 4 vertical layers
- **Internal Carton Dimensions:** 275 mm (W) × 530 mm (L) × 185 mm (H)
- **Gross Weight:** ~5.8 – 6.2 kg
- **Carton Grade:** 5-ply Double Wall corrugated board (B/C Flute 175/150 gsm)
