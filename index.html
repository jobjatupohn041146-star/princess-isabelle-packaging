🔬 30 ml Serum Bulge Engineering

A 30 ml volume sealed in a 9×12 cm pouch naturally creates a central cushion bulge of 9.5 – 11 mm. When housed with the dry silk sheet in the 12×16 cm sachet, each finished set measures ~11.5 mm.

✨ The Royal Fresh-Mix Ritual

Isolating the 30 ml active serum ensures peak potency and vitality of golden silk fibroin peptides until the moment of application.

1

Unveil The Box

Pull the champagne gold satin ribbon to glide open the drawer and retrieve one dual-sachet set.

2

Reveal Components

Tear along the notches of the 12×16 cm outer sachet to extract the dry silk mask and the inner 9×12 cm serum pouch.

3

Activate Fresh Serum

Tear the serum pouch at the directional notch and pour the 30 ml active formula evenly over the dry silk mask to activate within 10 seconds.

4

Radiate & Glow

Smooth the drenched silk mask onto cleansed face for 15-20 minutes of royal pampering and luminous skin renewal.
