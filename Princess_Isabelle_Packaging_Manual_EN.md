## 2. Dimensional & Volumetric Engineering

### 2.1 3-Sachet Stack Height Calculation
- Each 120 × 160 mm sachet containing the soaked mask sheet and 25g/30ml serum bulges ~9.5 – 10.5 mm at the center.
- **Stacked Height for 3 Sachets**: **~30 – 31.5 mm** (~3 cm).
- Net tray cavity depth of **33 mm (> 3 cm)** perfectly contains the stack with a 1.5 mm clearance for seamless sliding.

### 2.2 Inner Hollow-Wall Tray Dimensions
- **Internal Cavity Dimensions**: **130 mm (W) × 170 mm (L) × 33 mm (D)** (Depth > 3 cm).
- **External Finished Tray Dimensions**:
  - Width: 130 + 10 + 10 = **150 mm**.
  - Length: 170 + 10 + 10 = **190 mm**.
  - Outer Height: 33 mm + 1.2 mm base = **34 mm**.
- **Clearance Margins**:
  - Cavity provides 5 mm clearance around 120 × 160 mm sachets.
  - Net depth of 33 mm easily houses the 31.5 mm 3-sachet stack with 1.5 mm top clearance.

### 2.3 Outer Slide Sleeve Dimensions
- **Finished External Dimensions**: **153 mm (W) × 193 mm (L) × 37 mm (H)**.
- **Internal Sleeve Clearance**: **151 mm (W) × 191 mm (L) × 35 mm (H)**.
- Delivers a 0.5–1.0 mm perimeter gliding tolerance for luxurious sliding feel.

## 4. Sheet Mask Ritual Flow
1. **Unveil**: Pull the gold ribbon on the short top edge to slide out the inner tray.
2. **Tear & Extract**: Tear the 12×16 cm sachet along the V-notch and retrieve the drenched golden silk mask sheet.
3. **Apply Royal Mask**: Smooth mask onto cleansed face, relaxing for 15-20 minutes.
4. **Radiate**: Remove mask and massage remaining serum into skin. No rinsing required.
