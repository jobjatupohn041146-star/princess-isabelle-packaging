## 2. Dimensional & Volumetric Engineering

### 2.1 30 ml Liquid Displacement & Bulge Calculation
- 30 ml liquid inside a 90 × 120 mm pouch creates a pillow-like center expansion of **9.5 – 11 mm**.
- Combined with the dry silk sheet inside the 120 × 160 mm outer sachet, each finished unit measures **11 – 12.5 mm** in center thickness.
- **Stacked Height for 3 Sets**: **33 – 36 mm**.

### 2.2 Inner Drawer Tray Dimensions
- **Internal Cavity**: **126 mm (W) × 168 mm (L) × 38 mm (D)**
- **Clearance Margins**:
  - Width: 120 mm sachet + 3 mm lateral tolerance on each side = 126 mm.
  - Length: 160 mm sachet + 4 mm longitudinal tolerance on each side = 168 mm.
  - Depth: 33-36 mm stack + 2-4 mm vertical clearance = 38 mm (prevents binding or box wall deformation).

### 2.3 Outer Rigid Sleeve Dimensions
- **Finished External Dimensions**: **132 mm (W) × 174 mm (L) × 43 mm (H)**
- **Internal Sleeve Clearance**: **128 mm (W) × 170 mm (L) × 40 mm (H)** (0.5 mm glide margin).
